# NeoN Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to creating a welcoming and respectful NeoN community that is free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, background, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, or experience level.

This code of conduct applies to all participants, including users, contributors, and maintainers.

## Expected behavior

- Respect and consideration towards others.
- Openness to questions and contributions from individuals of all experience levels.
- Constructive feedback that focuses on the work rather than the person, and respectful acceptance of feedback.
- Technical discussions based on evidence and reasoned arguments.
- Respect for the privacy, boundaries, and time of others.
- Recognition of the contributions of your peers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Insults, personal attacks, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Encouraging or supporting any of the behaviors listed above.

## Scope

This code applies to all NeoN project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other channels of project communication. It also applies when someone officially represents NeoN in external spaces, such as conferences, workshops, or online events.

We will acknowledge your report within 7 days. Reports will be handled sensitively, and information will only be shared with those who need it to assess and address the concern.

If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for applying this code fairly and consistently. Anyone directly involved in a complaint must step aside from handling it.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

1. A private request for the individual to change their behavior.
2. Removal or editing of inappropriate content.
3. Issuing a formal warning.
4. Imposing a temporary restriction on participation in project spaces.
5. Enforcing a permanent ban from the project.

The response will consider the severity and impact of the behavior, as well as whether it has been repeated. Serious incidents may lead directly to a temporary or permanent ban.

## Appeals

If you disagree with a decision, you can request a review by maintainers who were not involved in the original decision. Please send your request to the reporting address above within 30 days of the decision.

## Updates

This code may be updated periodically. Changes will occur through the standard review process in the NeoN repository.
