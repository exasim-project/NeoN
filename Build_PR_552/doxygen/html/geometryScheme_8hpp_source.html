42/* @class GeometryScheme
43 * @brief Implements access to compute weights and nonOrthDeltaCoeffs

45 * Where:

48 * - nonOrthDeltaCoeff: 1 / (faceNormal . cellToCellDist), the over-relaxed (non-orthogonal)
